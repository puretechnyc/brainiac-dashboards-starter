Team

Client assignments

Tick the staff who work on a client, then save. Saving replaces the whole set for that client. An assignment says who works on an account — it is not billing, and it does not change what anybody can see in tt.

Client access requests

You can only log time against a client you are assigned to. Ask for the client here: an admin or a manager approves the request, and the approval assigns you to it.

Weekly hour budgets

A budget is how many hours a person may work in a week, Monday to Sunday Eastern. Only work hours count — breaks, time off, sick days and holidays do not. When the budget is reached, clock-in is blocked for the rest of that week; an admin can grant more hours and unlock it.

User Management

Departments

The agency-wide list the team logs hours against. Every client uses the same departments. Archiving keeps past hours but removes a department from the Log hours dropdown; renaming keeps all past hours on it.

Lock Entries Before Date

Lock all time entries before a specific date. This approves and locks all entries, making them immutable.

Client Team Weights

Set per-member hour weights for each client. A weight of 3:1 means 3 logged hours count as 1 allotment hour.
